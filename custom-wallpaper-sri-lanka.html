Single-Piece, Seamless Wallpaper

ARTYHOMES specializes in single-piece wall murals – one continuous printed panel sized to your wall, with no repeating pattern and no visible joins. This is ideal for a bold feature wall in a living room, café or hotel lobby where a seamless image matters more than a repeat pattern.

How Custom Wallpaper Pricing Works

Unlike standard imported rolls sold in fixed widths, ARTYHOMES prints every custom wallpaper design to your wall's exact dimensions and charges per sq ft (or per sq m on request), so you only pay for the wall area you actually cover. See our price guide for material options and typical ranges.
