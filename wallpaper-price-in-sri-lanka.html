What is included in the price?

Our starting price of LKR 350 per sq ft includes installation and a 6-year warranty. Every wallpaper is printed to your exact wall size, so you only pay for the wall area you actually cover.

What affects the wallpaper price in Sri Lanka?

The main factors are the material — vinyl, non-woven, textured or peel & stick — the complexity of the design, and the size of your wall. A custom mural with a detailed image costs more than a simple pattern, and a larger wall costs more than a small feature wall.

How to get an exact quote

Send your wall measurements in sq ft or sq m on WhatsApp and we will send a free, no-obligation quote. We deliver island-wide across Sri Lanka and can also arrange export orders.
