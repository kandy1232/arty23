Custom Wallpaper, Made to Your Wall

ARTYHOMES is a wallpaper shop in Colombo creating customized wallpaper and wall murals for homes, cafés, hotels, gyms and offices across Sri Lanka, with a refined, European-inspired design sensibility. We custom print every design and quote on a per sq ft basis according to your exact wall size – no fixed rolls, no wasted material, no awkward joins. Browse living room wallpaper, brick wallpaper, peel and stick wallpaper, 3D wall murals and modern wallpaper designs, or upload your own artwork for a fully personalized wall mural.
